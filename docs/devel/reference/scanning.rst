Scan experiments
================

The experiment classes are documented in :doc:`experiments`.
Their ``axes``, ``axis_parameter_names``, ``get_position_indices`` and shared
Concert parameters come from ``ScanMixin``. Runtime-installed parameters such
as ``scan_x_values`` depend on the instance's axis names and are described in
:ref:`parameter-scanning` rather than generated by autodoc.
